# Hero motion generation experiment

Built-in image_gen; original identity reference: public/art/hero-v2.png.
Both results are rejected candidates, not runtime assets. No billable cost estimate was available.

## Attempt 1

Create a production animation SOURCE SHEET for the exact teal-shirt young male swordsman shown in the attached reference atlas. The reference atlas is IDENTITY AND ART STYLE reference only: preserve his face, short brown spiky hair, teal blue short-sleeve tunic, brown belt, brown trousers, leather boots and slim steel sword. Match the reference compact low-poly rendered 3D cartoon, NOT pixel art, not a different costume. Output one 1536x1024 sheet containing exactly EIGHT poses in a strict 4 columns by 2 rows equal-cell grid, reading left to right top row then bottom row. No grid lines, labels, shadows, text, borders, floor or effects. Entire background must be flat pure #FF00FF magenta. Every full-body figure fits inside its cell, feet grounded on the same baseline, equal identity/head/body size and locked orthographic 3/4 side camera facing SCREEN RIGHT. Character is jogging steadily IN PLACE, sword held LOW safely in his RIGHT hand pointing forward/down and never swinging as an attack. Eight consecutive distinct phases of one seamless jog cycle: (1) left foot front contact right foot behind (2) left leg down/weight bearing right foot lifts (3) left foot under hips right knee passing forward (4) left foot behind pushing off right knee forward (5) RIGHT foot front contact LEFT foot behind (6) right leg down/weight bearing left foot lifts (7) right foot under hips left knee passing forward (8) right foot behind pushing off left knee forward, about to return to frame 1. Visible alternating legs, bent knees, light weight shift, opposite-arm motion; keep sword in same hand, readable separation of both legs. Root centered at same horizontal cell position and same ground level. Do not duplicate the same stride pose eight times. Both contact poses are opposite legs, and passing poses have feet nearly under body. Moderate motion rather than exaggerated leaps. No cut-off hair, sword tips or feet. Each cell has generous clean magenta margins. This is an animation sequence, not eight character design variants.

## Attempt 2 (edit of attempt 1)

Edit this eight-frame running sprite sheet. Keep the EXACT character, camera, scale, eight-cell layout and style. Correct the RUN CYCLE ONLY: top row is near-leg stance half-cycle, bottom row MUST BE opposite FAR-leg stance half-cycle, not a repeat of the top row. Eight equally spaced phases: near-foot forward contact / near-foot weight down, far heel lifting / near-foot below hip, far knee passes it forward / near-foot behind at toe-off, far shin extends / FAR-foot forward contact with NEAR-foot behind / far-foot weight down with near heel lifting / far-foot below hip, near knee passes forward / far-foot behind at toe-off with near shin extends. Make the far leg visibly darker so alternating legs are unmistakable. Arms counter-swing slightly; sword stays in same near hand held low. No camera rotation, body-size changes, stride translation, or hovering feet. Keep each character full-body inside its own cell. Use actual transparent background, with NO backdrop, NO glow or atmosphere, NO floor shadow. Maintain pure transparent empty pixels around silhouettes. Output 4 columns x 2 rows 1536x1024, no text or grids.
